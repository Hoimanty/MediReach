About & emergency contacts

In a life-threatening emergency call 999 first. This website is a student prototype and cannot dispatch help.

The problem

During a medical emergency, families lose valuable minutes phoning hospitals one by one to ask whether a bed, ambulance or blood group is available.

What this project does

It starts from the emergency need, takes your location, checks availability, and shows the nearest available result with one-tap call and directions.

How to use it in an emergency

  1. Call 999 if the patient is in immediate danger.
  2. Choose what you need on the Home page and set your location.
  3. Pick the top Available result, then Call to confirm before you travel.

Important disclaimer

All hospitals, ambulances, blood stocks, doctors and phone numbers are fictional demo data with simulated availability. A real system would need verified providers, live updates, a secure backend and audit logs.
